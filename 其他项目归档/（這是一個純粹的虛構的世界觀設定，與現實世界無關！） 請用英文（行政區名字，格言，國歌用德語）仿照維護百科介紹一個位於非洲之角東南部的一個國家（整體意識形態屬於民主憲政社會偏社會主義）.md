### Geography

Tserena occupies the southeastern projection of the Horn of Africa, bordering the Guardafui Channel and the Indian Ocean. The terrain shifts from arid coastal plains to basalt plateaus and rift-valley highlands in the west. The Haddar River system supports inland agriculture; the coast contains coral estuaries and salt flats.

Major regions:

- **Haddar Coast** — capital and port zone
- **Aroun Highlands** — electoral heartland, cooperative farms
- **Selan Depression** — geothermal energy zone
- **Mireb Steppe** — pastoralist reserves

### Politics and ideology

Tserena is a **democratic constitutional social republic**. The 1979 Constitutional Charter blends:

- liberal civil liberties,
- multi-party elections,
- strong labour cooperatives,
- universal public healthcare and education,
- land held in civic trust (not full private commodification).

The state is explicitly **not Marxist-Leninist**; it describes itself as *“market-social democracy under constitutional limits.”* Private enterprise exists, but strategic sectors (energy, water, ports, health) are publicly governed.

Key institutions:

- **Constitutional Court** — guards rights and electoral integrity
- **Citizens' Assembly** — 240 seats, proportional + local assembly delegates
- **Civic Chamber** — non-party body representing unions, cooperatives, women's councils, youth councils
- **Public Trust Authority** — manages natural resources and public infrastructure

Political culture stresses *deliberation, not domination*.

**Constitutional Charter of the Republic of Tserena**
**Preamble — Draft Text**
*(Official languages: Tseren and English; ceremonial version includes German)*

---

We, the citizens of Tserena — free men and free women of the coast, the highlands, the steppe, and the rift —

Conscious of the blood shed in the War of the Salt Winds, and of the long darkness before the First Civic Convention gathered in Haddar;

Mindful that no crown, no clan, no corporation, and no party shall ever again claim the right to own the sea, the soil, or the soul of this land;

Affirming that liberty without equality is privilege, and equality without liberty is bondage — and that both, without solidarity, perish in the dust;

Do hereby ordain and establish this Constitutional Charter, as the supreme law of the Republic of Tserena.

---

**Article I — The Foundation**

§1. Tserena is a sovereign, secular, democratic, and social republic.
§2. All power derives from the people, who exercise it through free elections, civic assemblies, and the institutions of cooperative self-government.
§3. The Republic rejects absolutism in every form — of capital, of office, of arms, and of creed. No single interest may dominate the common good.

---

**Article II — Rights and Duties**

§4. Every person born on or naturalised into Tserena possesses inalienable rights: to life, to dignity, to speech, to assembly, to belief, and to the fruits of their labour.
§5. The Republic guarantees:
 (a) universal healthcare, free at point of service;
 (b) education, free through the highest level of attainment;
 (c) housing as a civic right, not a commodity;
 (d) meaningful work, or public support in its absence.
§6. With these rights come duties: to vote, to serve on juries and civic bodies when called, to defend the Constitution, and to leave the coast, the soil, and the water cleaner than one found them.

---

**Article III — The Economic Order**

§7. Land, subsoil, waters, and the continental shelf are held in civic trust. They may be leased, but never alienated.
§8. Strategic sectors — energy, water, ports, health, and communications — shall be governed by public or cooperative authority.
§9. Private enterprise is permitted and protected, provided it serves the common good and does not concentrate power to the injury of democracy.
§10. Worker councils shall exist in every enterprise of fifty or more employees, with codetermination rights proportional to scale.

---

**Article IV — Governance**

§11. The Republic is governed by the Constitutional Charter, interpreted by the Constitutional Court, whose justices are elected by supermajority of the Citizens' Assembly and may be removed only by popular recall.
§12. The Citizens' Assembly shall be elected every four years by universal suffrage, under a mixed system of proportional and local representation.
§13. The Civic Chamber shall represent the organised voice of labour, cooperatives, women's councils, youth councils, and regional assemblies. It shall possess suspensive veto over legislation affecting social and economic rights.
§14. The President is the guardian of the Charter, not the master of the state. No president shall serve more than two terms.

---

**Article V — The Motto and the Anthem**

§15. The motto of the Republic is *Freiheit, Gleichheit, Solidarität*. It shall be taught in every school and inscribed in every public hall.
§16. The national anthem, *„Lied der Küste“*, shall be sung on every day of civic remembrance, and may be sung in German on all ceremonial occasions.

---

**Article VI — Foreign Relations**

§17. Tserena shall be a neutral state, offering its ports for humanitarian relief and mediation, and never for aggression.
§18. The Republic shall stand in solidarity with all peoples struggling for self-determination, democracy, and social justice, without interference in their internal affairs.

---

**In witness whereof**, we set our hands and seals upon this Charter, on the fifteenth day of Hamra, in the year of the New Calendar two thousand and twenty-four, in the city of New Haddar, before the wave and the wind.

---

*Drafted by the Constitutional Convention of Tserena, 47th Session*
*Ratified by popular referendum, 12 August 2024 (78.3% majority)*
*Entered into force, 1 January 2025*
