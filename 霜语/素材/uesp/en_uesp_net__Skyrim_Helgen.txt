It is the location where you begin the game, as you are transported there to be executed. Largely run by the [[Skyrim:Imperial Legion|Imperial military]], the town features several fortified towers and buildings. Shortly after your arrival, the settlement comes under attack by the ancient [[Skyrim:Dragon|dragon]] [[Skyrim:Alduin|Alduin]], and you are forced to escape through [[Skyrim:Helgen Keep|Helgen Keep]].

If you return to the town after visiting [[Skyrim:Whiterun|Whiterun]], the ruins will be inhabited by a group of [[Skyrim:Bandit|bandits]].

==Notes==
* During the introductory sequence before the initial quest [[Skyrim:Unbound|Unbound]], [[Skyrim:Ralof|Ralof]] remembers [[Skyrim:Vilod|Vilod]], who used to make [[Skyrim:Mead with Juniper Berry|mead with juniper berries]]. If you later return to Helgen, bottles of this mead may be found on the second floor of the partly destroyed inn you jumped into during Unbound.
* Two additional residents are present during the introductory sequence; these unnamed NPCs are referenced only as <code>MQ101GenericCivilianNordMale</code> and <code>MQ101GenericCivilianNordFemale</code> in the game data. They're seen carrying firewood and a bucket inside the gate when the carriage enters town, but are never seen again.
* Helgen doesn't count as a discovered location, and therefore won't count toward the [[Skyrim:Explorer|Explorer]] achievement.
* Helgen is the default destination for bad AI targets, meaning that bugged NPCs may end up wandering nearby.
* Approximately halfway down the road to [[Skyrim:Pinewatch|Pinewatch]], a small group of bandits has set up camp under an overhanging ledge. The bandits guard a novice-locked chest and a copy of the [[Skyrim:Light Armor|Light Armor]] [[Skyrim:Skill Books|skill book]] ''[[Skyrim:The Refugees|The Refugees]]''.
* The locked gate, bandits, barrels, chest, potions, mead bottles, Cast Iron Pot, Wooden Bowl, Kettle, Basket, Wooden Ladle and Tankard respawn; burnt corpses, the wardrobe, and the end table don't respawn.
*The double bed at the inn is considered “owned”; however, if you choose to escape Helgen with Hadvar, you will be allowed to sleep in the bed after becoming friends with his uncle Alvor.
*This wiki's current Skyrim satellite map displays Helgen in its original state; the previous one displayed Helgen in its destroyed state.
