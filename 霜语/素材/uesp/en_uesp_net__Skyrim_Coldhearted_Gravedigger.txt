The '''Coldhearted Gravedigger''' is a [[Skyrim:Nord|Nord]] [[Skyrim:Warrior (class)|warrior]] who may appear in the [[Skyrim:Hall of the Dead (Windhelm)|Hall of the Dead]] in [[Skyrim:Windhelm|Windhelm]] during the quest [[Skyrim:The Dark Brotherhood Forever|The Dark Brotherhood Forever]]. She is one of ten possible targets.

The Gravedigger wears farm [[Skyrim:Clothes#000261c0|clothes]] along with a pair of [[Skyrim:Boots#000261bd|boots]] and a pair of [[Skyrim:Gloves#000261c1|gloves]]. She wields an [[Skyrim:Iron Dagger|iron dagger]] in combat.
