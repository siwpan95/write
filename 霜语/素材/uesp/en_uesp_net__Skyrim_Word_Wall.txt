A '''word wall''' is a wall etched with a set of words in the [[Lore:Dragon Language|Dragon Language]].  Multiple word walls can be found in various locations throughout Skyrim.

On each word wall one of these words may be highlighted.  A highlighted word is a [[Skyrim:Word of Power|word of power]], that allows you to unlock one word in one of the [[SR:Dragon Shouts|Dragon Shouts]]. Once you have found a word of power you must absorb a [[Skyrim:Dragon|Dragon]] soul to use the associated Dragon Shout.

==Locations==

Most of the word walls are located in [[Skyrim:Dragon Lairs|Dragon Lairs]] and [[Skyrim:Nordic Ruins|Nordic Ruins]]. There are two quests that help you discover new word walls:
{{#define:radiantlinks|false}}
*{{Quest Link|The Words of Power}}
*{{Quest Link|Shout - learn about word wall}}
You can only obtain one location at a time from each method, but you can use both methods at once. Neither source will ever direct you to a location that is potentially quest-locked (even if the quest is active), or to locations that are missing from their list ([[Skyrim:Frostmere Crypt|Frostmere Crypt]] and [[Skyrim:Shalidor's Maze|Shalidor's Maze]]).

==Word Wall Translations==
The text shown on the word wall depends on how many words you've learned for that shout. For example, if you have already learned the first word in the shout [[Skyrim:Aura Whisper|Aura Whisper]] in [[Skyrim:Volunruud|Volunruud]], then if you visit either [[Skyrim:Northwind Summit|Northwind Summit]] or [[Skyrim:Valthume|Valthume]], the word wall will be for the second word. This means that the word wall's text changes, depending on which Word(s) you've already learned; the two exceptions are [[Skyrim:Throw Voice|Throw Voice]] ([[Skyrim:Shearpoint|Shearpoint]]) and [[Skyrim:Unrelenting Force|Unrelenting Force]] ([[Skyrim:Bleak Falls Barrow (place)|Bleak Falls Barrow]]).

==Notes==
* The choral part of the "Word Learned" sound effect is considered to be music and will not be heard if your music volume is set to zero.
* There are two ruined word walls that you are unable to learn from; one in [[Skyrim:Sovngarde (place)|Sovngarde]] and one in the [[Skyrim:Soul Cairn|Soul Cairn]].{{DG}}
* The "word wall" for Drain Vitality located in the Forgotten Vale is not a traditional ancient Nordic structure, but rather a large boulder with a single word carved into it.{{DG}}
