==Quick Walkthrough==
# Find [[Online:Skaldir|Skaldir]] at the [[Online:Mages Guild (Mournhold)|Mages Guild]] in [[Online:Mournhold|Mournhold]].
# Locate and warn the other scryers in [[Online:Shornhelm|Shornhelm]], [[Online:Marbruk|Marbruk]], and [[Online:Stormhold|Stormhold]].
# Talk to [[Online:Lilatha|Lilatha]].
# Recover the Anuic Lantern from [[Online:Khenarthi's Roost|Khenarthi's Roost]] and the Liminal Lens from [[Online:The Howling Sepulchers|the Howling Sepulchers]].
# Find Lilatha's boat in [[Online:Vulkhel Guard|Vulkhel Guard]] and make a decision about [[Online:Bonafryd|Bonafryd]].
# Travel to [[Online:Dranil Kir|Dranil Kir]] and find Lilatha.
# Defend Lilatha
# Reassemble and activate the Obscuros.
# Talk to Lilatha.

==Detailed Walkthrough==
===A Vision of Obscurity===
[[File:ON-quest-Of Knives and Long Shadows.jpg|thumb|right|Skaldir, stabbed to death by his own shadow]]
To begin this quest, locate the ''[[Online:Order of the Eye Dispatch|Order of the Eye Dispatch]]'' at [[Online:Canton of St. Delyn the Wise|Mages Guild hall in Vivec]]. It hints at a shocking discovery made by scryers of the Order of the Eye, but fails to go into much more detail. To find out more, you must seek out [[Online:Skaldir|Skaldir]] at the [[Online:Mages Guild (Mournhold)|Mournhold Mages Guild]].

When you arrive, he is busy examining a bookshelf, but turns as you approach:
:''"You read my letter? Oh, I'm so grateful you've come! We found something powerful. A Daedric—wait, what's happening?"''
Unfortunately his explanation is cut off, as at that moment, his shadow comes to life and stabs him to death. [[Online:Bonafryd|Bonafryd]] runs over and cries out in frustration.
:'''Bonafryd:''' ''"Skaldir! No!"''
:'''Bonafryd:''' ''"Damn it! Who's doing this? I'll send them straight to [[Lore:Sovngarde|Sovngarde]]!"''
You must talk to her to ascertain what is going on. Apparently a number of the scryers have been looking into some obscure Daedric artifact. Bonafryd wants you to warn the other scryers before they suffer the same fate as Skaldir. [[Online:Adeber Loumont|Adeber Loumont]] can be found in [[Online:Shornhelm|Shornhelm]], [[Online:Wenridil|Wenridil]] is in [[Online:Marbruk|Marbruk]], and [[Online:Neelo|Neelo]] can be found in [[Online:Stormhold|Stormhold]].

===Warning the Scryers===
'''Adeber''': Head to the city of [[Online:Shornhelm|Shornhelm]] in [[Online:Rivenspire|Rivenspire]]. At the [[Online:Mages Guild (Shornhelm)|Mages Guild]], you come across [[Online:Eloic Montieu|Eloic Montieu]]. If you have completed the Mages Guild questline, he will recognize you and offer his assistance. If not, he will be somewhat wary. Either way, once you mention that his life is in danger, he informs you that Adeber has stepped outside for some quiet. You can find him behind Merethrin's House, slumped against a barrel, already dead. Collect [[Online:Adeber's Journal|his journal]]. {{Item Link|Adeber's Notebook|questid=6273}} will be in your Quest Inventory if you wish to learn more about his scrying techniques. At this point, you have the opportunity to return to Eloic Montieu to deliver the sad news. Once that is done, you can move on.

'''Wenridil''': Head to the city of [[Online:Marbruk|Marbruk]] in [[Online:Greenshade|Greenshade]]. Wenridil can be found on the patio behind the [[Online:Queen's Residence|Queen's Residence]]. She moves off as you approach, accusing you of leering at her. At the same time, her [[Online:Wenridil's Shadow|shadow]] comes to life and starts attacking, but she manages to escape. You will need to defeat the shadow and catch up with her. Inside the [[Online:Mages Guild (Marbruk)|Mages Guild]], Wenridil can be found hiding behind a plant pot and will cower when spoken to.
:'''Are you all right?'''
:''"Not really. I think my shadow may have peeled off some of my [[Online:Souls|animus]]. My soul, I mean. Or maybe just some of my magical aptitude. Either way, it's something I'll miss.''
:''Don't blame yourself. You did it keep it from killing me, after all."''

After this, she says something that may bring up a little warning bell in your mind, and then she points you to [[Online:Wenridil's Logbook|her logbook]].
:'''Bonafryd sent me. She thinks these attacks have something to do with the artifact you were studying.'''
:''"Well, that was charitable of her. Didn't think she much liked me. Bit of a sorehead, that one. Anyway, I'm grateful.
:''I hid my notes on the artifact in the planter over there - just in case, you know? They're yours if you want them."''

The logbook you find in the planter, {{Item Link|Wenridil's Notebook|questid=6274}}, has some interesting glimpses of the artifact the group has been scrying.

'''Neelo''': Head to the city of [[Online:Stormhold|Stormhold]] in [[Online:Shadowfen|Shadowfen]]. Neelo is tucked between some big rocks down by the river, just east of [[Online:Stormhold Wayshrine|Stormhold Wayshrine]]. He threatens you to stay away, saying he will not die like the others and that, if you approach, his wards will tear you apart. You can either [[Online:Intimidating Presence|intimidate]] him into lowering his wards or locate his sister, [[Online:Captain Xarei|Xarei]], who may be able to convince him. She can be found by the market stalls in the center of the city and, upon telling her the situation, you can lead her to Neelo to convince him. When you approach, she will speak with him briefly. 
:'''Captain Xarei:''' ''"Easy does it, Neelo. It is me. What troubles you this time?"''
:'''Neelo:''' ''"Egg-sister? What are you doing with that … that shadow tamer?"''
:'''Captain Xarei:''' ''"Do not be a withered root. This one means you no harm, and I am here to protect you now, all right? So lower your wards."''
:'''Neelo:''' ''"I … yes, sister."''

Once Neelo has been convinced (one way or another) to lower his wards, he will direct you to [[Online:Neelo's Notes|his journal]], located at the root of a nearby tree, so that you may take them "far, far away." If you fetched his sister, he also apologizes for his paranoia. Taking a close look at {{Item Link|Neelo's Notes|questid=6275}} in your quest items inventory, you get a glimmer about who is behind these attacks:
:''"I saw the water-woman again today ... cloaked in shadow ... beneath a black hood ... she seems cold ..."''

===The Obscuros===
Once you have found all three scryers, a projection of an Altmer named [[Online:Lilatha|Lilatha]] appears, speaking to you.
:'''Lilatha:''' ''"You, who would unravel the secret of these murderous shadows. Approach me."''
Talk to her and she will explain that she is a member of the Psijic Order, and that she has also been looking into the same Daedric artifact that the scryers have seen. In order for you to find out where the artifact is located, she explains, you will need to collect both an Anuic Lantern from a shipwreck on [[Online:Khenarthi's Roost|Khenarthi's Roost]] and a {{Item Link|Liminal Lens|questid=6277}} from [[Online:The Howling Sepulchers|the Howling Sepulchers]] in [[Online:Craglorn|Craglorn]]. If questioned, she will explain that they are needed to build a Psijic scrying device known as The Obscuros.

To find the {{Item Link|Anuic Lantern|questid=6276}}, head south of [[Online:Eagle's Strand|Eagle's Strand]] on Khenarthi's Roost. Just south of a group of sailors is the shipwreck; upon checking inside the cabin, you will find an open chest and a dead bandit holding a [[Online:Soggy Note|soggy note]]. The note urges you to get revenge for the author and reveals that the lantern has been taken to [[Online:Hazak's Hollow|Hazak's Hollow]] on the other side of the island. The lantern can be found in a freshly packed crate on the west side of the cave, near the back. 

The {{Item Link|Liminal Lens|questid=6277}} is located inside the Ancient Priest's Coffer in the Dragon Priest's Burial Chamber at the far end of the Howling Sepulchers. From the entrance, head north along the ravine bridge and turn to the west once you leave the cavernous passage. Again, head through a small cave and cross another bridge. From there, you will need to enter another cave; this time, you will end up in a Nordic barrow. You can ignore the necromancer, [[Online:Rosathild|Rosathild]], pretty easily. Heading east, you will need to cross yet another bridge over the ravine. Continue in roughly a straight line along the path, up the stairs, and to the Dragon Priest's Burial Chamber. Unlike before, you can't avoid the boss, [[Online:Akiirdal|Akiirdal]], since they are directly behind the coffer. Once defeated, you can open the Ancient Priest's Coffer and take the Liminal Lens.

Once you have collected both items, head to [[Online:Vulkhel Guard|Vulkhel Guard]], where Lilatha's boat is waiting. It can be found on the far side of the outer harbor wall, but, before boarding, Bonafryd appears and rushes over.
:'''Bonafryd:''' ''"Wait! Don't leave yet!"''

===The Isle of Dranil Kir===
[[File:ON-quest-Of Knives and Long Shadows 03.jpg|thumb|right|A projection of Sotha Sil's Clockwork City]]
Apparently, before you left Mournhold, Bonafryd slipped a coin with a tracking charm on it into your pocket. She wants to go with you, since she thinks it will be too dangerous otherwise. You need to decide whether or not to bring her along.

If you decide against it, she sends you on your way and you arrive at [[Online:Dranil Kir|Dranil Kir]] alone and in the rain. If you agree to bring Bonafryd on the boat, she arrives with you but gets sea sick and tells you to go on ahead. Either way, head up the path to find Lilatha warming her hands at a fire as she waits for you.
:'''Lilatha:''' ''"Campfire on the beach …. I still don't see the appeal."''
:'''Lilatha:''' ''"Ah, you've arrived! Welcome to Dranil Kir. Let's get out of the rain, shall we?"''
:'''Lilatha:''' ''"I trust you brought the items I requested. The scrying chamber is just up the hill. Follow me."''

She runs off up a hill and into the Obscuros Scrying Chamber, an underground room in the nearby Ayleid ruins. Lilatha heads towards the podium, making idle conversation as she does.
:'''Lilatha:''' ''"I'm confident that the components you brought will it [sic], but the Obscuro's device has been dormant for centuries."''
:'''<Lilatha stops in front of the Obscuros.>'''
:'''Lilatha:''' ''"Do you feel that chill? It's like … oh no."''
:'''<Lilatha freezes awkwardly, shadows surrounding her. She struggles, but is unable to move.>'''
:'''Lilatha:''' ''"What's happening to me?"''
:'''<[[Online:Lilatha's Shadow|Lilatha's shadow]] appears behind her.>'''
:'''Lilatha:''' ''"You must …. kill the shadow. Hurry!"'' 

Her shadow has similar attacks to the one you fought previously. It must be defeated.

Suddenly, Bonafryd calls out. Her dialogue changes depending on whether you took her with you or not, but, no matter the choice, she will appear and threaten the pair of you. She enters the way you came, wearing a [[Online:Crafting Motif 57: Ebonshadow Style|dark, shadowy set of armor]] and armed with a mace and shield. It turns out she is behind the shadow attacks. She summons another copy of [[Online:Lilatha's Shadow|Lilatha's Shadow]].
:'''Lilatha:''' ''"No! I will not perish here!"''

You must defeat Bonafryd in order to save both Lilatha and the device. During the battle, she yells angrily at you, and when she is close to death she cries out that she will "not fail [her] mistress." Once she and the second shadow are defeated, you are able to reassemble and activate the Obscuros.
:'''Lilatha:''' ''"Set the lens and the lantern in the Obscuros. Time to see what this has all been about."''

Place the lantern in its housing and mount the lens on top to rebuild it. Activate it. A series of golden projections appears, starting with an odd creature that resembles a [[Online:Clannfear|clannfear]].
:'''Lilatha:''' ''"A [[Online:Verminous Fabricant|fabricant]]? I don't …."''
:'''<The projection changes to one of the Clockwork City's external structure, the [[Online:Revolving Celestiodrome|Revolving Celestiodrome]].>'''
:'''Lilatha:''' ''"No, this cannot be. The city was sealed long ago."''
:'''<A [[Online:Shrike|woman]] in a long, roughly made skirt and brassiere appears, holding a staff.>'''
:'''Lilatha:''' ''"What is this creature? Some new Daedric beast?"''
:'''<The projections end.>'''
:'''Lilatha:''' ''"The [[Online:Clockwork City|Clockwork City]]. Could it be, after all this time …?"''

Talk to Lilatha for further interpretation of what the Obscuros has shown you. She will explain that this is likely a serious threat to all of Tamriel, and gives you a reward for your assistance. If you have completed the main questline of the Clockwork City, she will instead say the threat is most likely not over.

She also comments about Bonafryd.
:'''I guess [[Online:Bonafryd|Bonafryd]] was a powerful mage after all.'''
:''"So, you knew our attacker? Hmm. She was powerful indeed, and yet I sense that the strength was not her own. Her form was clumsy, but she struck with the power of a master wizard.''
:''Usually I'd suspect possession. But with her? I'm not so certain."''
:'''What do you mean?'''
:''"Someone granted her powers beyond her ken. An agent of [[Lore:Oblivion|Oblivion]], no doubt.''
:''Bonafryd was clearly filled with hatred. Consumed by jealousy or resentment, perhaps? The Daedra can twist those passions into untold horrors. I pity her."''

==Notes==
* You must be Level 15 or higher to receive this quest from the Vivec Mages Guild<!--, but the Crown Store quest starter can be used at any time-->.
* Prior to [[Online:Update 40|Update 40]], the [[Online:Order of the Eye Dispatch|Order of the Eye Dispatch]] could be found in every Mages Guild hall.
* As with other prologue quests, the events of this quest foreshadowed future events in the ESO quest timeline. In this case, the events of the [[Online:Clockwork City|Clockwork City]] DLC.
* Once this quest is completed, [[Online:Lilatha|Lilatha]] will be available to give the follow-on quest, [[Online:To The Clockwork City|To The Clockwork City]], although she will not start it automatically.
* If you complete the quest without the book being read, a copy can be found in the Bangkorai Garrison in Bangkorai.
* If you have a quest that requires you to kill a number of daedra, the shadows will count... as will Bonafryd!
* If the city guard comes across Wenridil's shadow, they will attack it. However, even if you normally turn off the option to attack innocents, you can still attack the guard if this happens.
