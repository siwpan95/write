'''Soul Split''' is an Apprentice level [[Skyrim:Conjuration|Conjuration]] spell added by the [[Skyrim:Necromantic_Grimoire|Necromantic Grimoire]] [[Skyrim:Creation Club|Creation]].
==Effects==
* Kills the target
* [[Skyrim:Conjure|Conjure  Sundered Shade]], for 60 secs

==Perks==
* [[Skyrim:Necromancy|Necromancy]], Greater duration for spell.
* [[Skyrim:Dark Souls|Dark Souls]], increases Health with 100.

==Notes== 
* Sundered Shades are immune to '''Soul Split'''.
* Sundered Shades spawn on the same spot making it look like there is only one until you move away.
*The Soul Split spell was inspired by a ''[[Legends:Legends|Legends]]'' card [[LG:Soul Split|of the same name]].
