The '''Base Cost''' values in this table can be used to determine the base cost of spells in the game, using the equation provided on the [[Oblivion:Spell Making|Spell Making]] page.  (Note, however, that a few built-in spells have arbitrary base costs that are not derived in this manner).

The '''Barter Factor''' is the increase a Constant Effect enchantment gives to an object's value per magnitude of the effect. So a Fortify Blunt 10pts enchantment will increase the cost of the item by 1000. Effects without a magnitude, such as [[Oblivion:Night Eye|Night Eye]], [[Oblivion:Water Breathing|Water Breathing]], and [[Oblivion:Water Walking|Water Walking]], use a fixed magnitude of 5 for the calculation.

* [[Oblivion:Magical Effects|Magical Effects]] provides details on these spell effects.
* [[Oblivion:Spells|Spells]] lists all the built-in spells, sorted by school and mastery level
* [[Oblivion:Leveled Spells|Leveled Spells]] lists the leveled spells that can be received on various quests.
* [[Oblivion:List of Spells by Effect|List of Spells by Effect]] lists all the built-in spells, similar to [[Oblivion:Spells|Spells]] but sorted by effect.
* See [[Oblivion:Magic|Magic]] for a comprehensive list of magic-related information.
