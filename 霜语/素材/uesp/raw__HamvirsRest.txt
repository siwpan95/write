==Walkthrough==
The graveyard is just north of [[Skyrim:Dustman's Cairn|Dustman's Cairn]]. Three skeletons patrol the area, and a draugr sleeps in the upright sarcophagus. A master-locked [[Skyrim:Chest|chest]] is beside the sarcophagus, with a random enchanted [[Skyrim:Helmet|helmet]] behind the grates to its left. Eight [[Skyrim:Nightshade|nightshade]] plants, two [[Skyrim:Purple Mountain Flower|purple mountain flowers]], and two [[Skyrim:Lavender|lavender]] plants grow among the tombstones and in the immediate area.

This is the final destination of the [[Skyrim:Headless Horseman|Headless Horseman]] on his nightly travels; once he reaches this area, he will disappear regardless of the time of day.

==Notes==
* An [[Skyrim:Iron Ore Vein|iron ore vein]] is west-southwest of the ruin.
