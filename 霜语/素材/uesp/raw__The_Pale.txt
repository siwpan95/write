It can also change sides as part of negotiations during [[Skyrim:Season Unending|Season Unending]].

The Pale is sparsely populated, with safe places for travelers few and far between. The snowy terrain that makes up most of the hold is unsuitable for farming; however, the far southern edge, where not mountainous, is more hospitable.

One of three entrances to [[Skyrim:Blackreach|Blackreach]] is in the depths of [[Skyrim:Raldbthar|Raldbthar]], a [[Skyrim:Dwarven Ruins|Dwarven ruin]] south of [[Skyrim:Lake Yorgrim|Lake Yorgrim]], the largest lake in the Pale. [[Skyrim:River Yorgrim|River Yorgrim]] flows from this lake.

==Flora and Fauna==
[[Skyrim:Snowberries|Snowberries]] thrive in the harsh, snow-covered environment, but the sturdy [[Skyrim:Blue Mountain Flower|blue]] and [[Skyrim:Purple Mountain Flower|purple]] variants of the mountain flower can also be found. Along the coast, [[Skyrim:Nordic Barnacle|Nordic barnacles]], [[Skyrim:Clam|clams]], and [[Skyrim:Spiky Grass|spiky grass]] dominate.

[[Skyrim:Giant|Giants]] are common here, as are the usual denizens of the snow: [[Skyrim:Snowy Sabre Cat|sabre cats]], [[Skyrim:Wolf|wolves]], [[Skyrim:Ice Wraith|ice wraiths]], [[Skyrim:Troll|trolls]], and [[Skyrim:Frostbite Spider|frostbite spiders]].
