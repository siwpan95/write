'''Dawnstar''' is a city on the northern coast of Skyrim, and the capital of [[Skyrim:The Pale|the Pale]]. It is initially aligned with the [[Skyrim:Stormcloaks|Stormcloaks]] under [[Skyrim:Jarl|Jarl]] [[Skyrim:Skald|Skald]]. The city is a relatively small fishing and mining settlement with few services. The residents will report having [[Skyrim:Waking Nightmare|horrible nightmares]], which some initially blame on a witch's curse.

For more information on Dawnstar, see the [[Lore:Dawnstar|lore article]].

==Notes==
*The scene with [[SR:Erandur|Erandur]] in the [[SR:Windpeak Inn|Windpeak Inn]] may be missed if you've talked to either [[SR:Brina Merilis|Brina Merilis]], [[SR:Skald|Skald]], [[SR:Madena|Madena]], [[SR:Rustleif|Rustleif]], [[SR:Frida|Frida]], [[SR:Beitild|Beitild]] or [[SR:Leigelf|Leigelf]] first in relation to the nightmares plaguing the town, and vice versa.
*With ''[[Skyrim:Hearthfire|Hearthfire]]'' installed, you can purchase [[Skyrim:Heljarchen Hall|a plot of land]] in the Pale to build a house on. Also, two [[Skyrim:Quarried Stone|stone quarries]] will be present.
*[[Skyrim:Dawnstar Sanctuary|Dawnstar Sanctuary]] is a [[Skyrim:Houses|house]] granted upon completion of the [[Skyrim:Dark Brotherhood|Dark Brotherhood]] quest [[Skyrim:Hail Sithis!|Hail Sithis!]]
*[[Skyrim:Dawnguard|Dawnguard]] adds a permanently disabled map marker for Dawnstar Docks.
*[[Skyrim:Quicksilver Mine|Quicksilver Mine]] is just south of Fruki's House, in the northwest corner of town.
*[[Skyrim:Iron-Breaker Mine|Iron-Breaker Mine]] is just east of [[Skyrim:The White Hall|The White Hall]], at the east end of town. 
*A blacksmith's forge and hide tanner are outside Rustleif's house, near the water.
*A tent is northeast of town, past the hill on which the lighthouse is situated. Inside, someone has set up a lovers' retreat, complete with two unowned bedrolls, a few articles of clothing, wine, [[Skyrim:Red Mountain Flower|flowers]], and an [[Skyrim:Amulet of Mara|amulet of Mara]].
*Dawnstar also appears in ''[[Arena:Dawnstar|Arena]]'' and ''[[Dawnstar:Places|Dawnstar]]''.
