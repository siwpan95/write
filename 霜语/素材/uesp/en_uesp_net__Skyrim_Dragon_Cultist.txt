'''Dragon Cultists''' are the restless spirits of members of the ancient [[Skyrim:Dragon_Cult|Dragon Cult]] who chose to take their own lives rather than fall to King Harald's army. They only appear to attack invaders in [[Skyrim:Forelhost|Forelhost]].

Dragon Cultists make use of [[Skyrim:Ancient Nord|ancient Nord]] weapons and armor, and may also carry a [[Skyrim:Dragon Priest Dagger|dragon priest dagger]]. Some Cultists are capable of using [[Skyrim:Destruction|Destruction]] magic.
