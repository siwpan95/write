'''Quests''' are sorted below by their type or starting location. There are 223 quests in the original base game (213 with journal entries and 10 without). ''[[Oblivion:Knights of the Nine|Knights of the Nine]]'' and the smaller [[Oblivion:Downloads|official downloads]] added 19 quests (18 with journal entries and 1 without). The ''[[Shivering:Shivering Isles|Shivering Isles]]'' expansion added 35 quests (33 with journal entries and 2 without). The Deluxe Edition of ''[[Oblivion:Oblivion Remastered|Oblivion Remastered]]'' added 4 quests.
