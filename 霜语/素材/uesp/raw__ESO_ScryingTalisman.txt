}}The '''Psijic Scrying Talisman''' was originally obtained through the purchase of either the physical or digital [[Online:Summerset Collector's Edition|Collector's Edition]] for [[ON:Summerset|Summerset]]. It is now available in the [[Online:Crown Store|Crown Store]] for {{ESO Crowns|2,100}} as part of the [[Online:Summerset Collector's Pack|Summerset Collector's Pack]].

Conjure a Psijic scrying circle.
