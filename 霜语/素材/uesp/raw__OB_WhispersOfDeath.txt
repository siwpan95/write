==Quick Walkthrough==
#Go to [[Oblivion:Bravil|Bravil]] once a week and activate the [[Lore:Night Mother|Night Mother]]'s statue to learn the details of somebody requiring the [[Oblivion:Dark Brotherhood|Dark Brotherhood]]'s services.
#Report these to [[Oblivion:Arquen|Arquen]] in the [[Oblivion:Dark Brotherhood Sanctuary|Dark Brotherhood Sanctuary]] and receive your reward for the previous week's kill.

==Detailed Walkthrough==
===The Lucky Old Lady===
Go to Bravil and activate the statue of the Lucky Old Lady as if you were trying to receive her usual blessing of luck. Instead, the Night Mother will tell you that somebody has prayed to her and that you should relay these details to your Speaker. You will receive random combinations of one of the following people and locations. None of these locations are in Cyrodiil, so you cannot contact the person in question yourself.

===Messenger of Death===
Head to the [[Oblivion:Cheydinhal|Cheydinhal]] sanctuary and speak to [[Oblivion:Arquen|Arquen]]. She will promise you that the matter will be taken care of, and hand over your earnings for the previous week.
