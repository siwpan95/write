'''Miscellaneous Items''' appear in your inventory under "Misc". It includes a wide variety of different types of items, many of which are detailed on other pages:
* [[Skyrim:Animal Hides|Animal Hides]]: hides, pelts, and leather
* [[Skyrim:Animal Parts|Animal Parts]]: bones, scales, tusks, skulls, etc.
* [[Skyrim:Gems|Gems]]: gems and gem-containing miscellaneous items
* [[Skyrim:Ores and Ingots|Ores and Ingots]]: ores, ingots, and scrap metal.
* [[Skyrim:Soul Gems|Soul Gems]]: soul gems used for [[Skyrim:Soul Trap (effect)|soul trapping]]
* [[Skyrim:Tools|Tools]]: lockpicks and other items categorized as tools by vendors

For items involved in quests, see [[Skyrim:Quest Items|Quest Items]].

All items added by the [[Skyrim:Add-ons|official add-ons]] have reference IDs that change, thus they are documented as "xx.....". More details on why this happens, and how to find them are found [[Skyrim:Form ID|here]].

==Bugs in a Jar==
These are unique live bugs captured in jars. Each jar also carries a unique inscription etched into the inside of the lid. These jars serve no apparent purpose (nor do their inscriptions have a known meaning), and there is no associated quest or reward for collecting them. However, [[Skyrim:Tundra Homestead (place)|Tundra Homestead]]'s cellar{{CC|Tundra Homestead}}, [[Skyrim:Myrwatch (place)|Myrwatch]]'s gallery{{CC|Myrwatch}}, and [[Skyrim:Shadowfoot Sanctum (place)|Shadowfoot Sanctum]]'s trophy room{{CC|Shadowfoot Sanctum}} contain a special holder for each one. If you tip the jars in any direction, the insects inside will always appear to remain in the same position. Additionally, the bee jar may emit a buzzing sound when you are nearby.

The butterfly, moth, and torchbug jars [[Skyrim:Respawn|respawn]] along with the areas where they are found, allowing multiple copies of these three jars to be obtained. The bee and dragonfly jars are in areas that do not respawn. With unofficial patch installed, the butterfly doesn't respawn anymore.

The [[Skyrim:Saints & Seducers|Saints & Seducers]] [[Skyrim:Creation Club|Creation]] adds three new bugs in a jar: a bliss bug, a green butterfly, and a purple butterfly.

==Children's Items{{HF}}==
After installing [[Skyrim:Hearthfire|Hearthfire]], you may be able to purchase certain [[Skyrim:Child|children's]] items from [[Skyrim:Radiant Raiment|Radiant Raiment]], general goods [[Skyrim:Merchants|merchants]], and [[Skyrim:Khajiit Traders|Khajiit traders]], such as apparel, a doll, or a [[Skyrim:Wooden Sword|wooden sword]].

These items appear in the Miscellaneous section of your inventory; you cannot wear the children's apparel yourself. However, if you [[Skyrim:Adoption|adopt]] a child, you can give them these items as a gift.

==Torches==
The duration of a torch is 240 seconds, and the radius of its light is 512. Other items, such as lanterns and candlesticks, cannot be used as sources of light. Torches, despite having a value, cannot be bought from or sold to any merchants, even with the Merchant perk. Torches given to followers will never burn out. Holding a torch improves [[Skyrim:Fortify Warmth|Warmth]] by 50 points when [[Skyrim:Survival Mode|Survival Mode]] is active.
