==Walkthrough==
The tower is along the main road heading west from Whiterun, allowing the [[Skyrim:Whiterun Guard|Whiterun guards]] in the area to watch for any potential threats approaching from that direction. It can be entered via the crumbling wall that forms a ramp upwards. Outside the tower the area between the fallen walls contains a training dummy with some Iron Arrows shot into it.  Inside the tower are two unowned bedrolls and a small table with a lantern. The second floor contains an archery target and a bale of straw, while the top floor is empty. There are two [[Skyrim:Imperial Legion|Imperial Legion]] banners flying from the tower.

Four Whiterun guards will be stationed at the tower to keep watch for enemies (see [[#Bugs|bugs]]).

Once the quest [[Skyrim:Dragon Rising|Dragon Rising]] is complete, the area between the fallen walls contains two burnt corpses with loot and the charred remains of a wooden hut.

==Notes==
* There are two [[Skyrim:Iron Ore Vein|iron ore veins]] in the rocks to the west.
* The Western Watchtower will not be marked as a location on your map until the quest Dragon Rising begins.
* The Imperial Legion banners will be replaced by the [[Skyrim:Stormcloaks|Stormcloak]] banners if the [[Skyrim:Battle for Whiterun (Stormcloaks)|Stormcloaks capture Whiterun]] during the [[Skyrim:Civil War|Civil War]] questline.
**Version 1.3.3 of the [[Skyrim Mod:Unofficial Skyrim Patch|Unofficial Skyrim Patch]] replaces the default Imperial Legion banners with yellow Whiterun banners to better reflect that Whiterun Hold is originally neutral in the Civil War questline.
*Version 4.1.1 of the [[Skyrim Mod:Unofficial Skyrim Special Edition Patch|Unofficial Skyrim Special Edition Patch]] replaces Western Watchtower's fort map icon with the one used for [[Skyrim:Towers|Imperial towers]].
*This wiki's current Skyrim satellite map displays Western Watchtower in its original state; the previous one displayed Western Watchtower in its damaged state.
