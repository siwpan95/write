Every '''soul''' in Oblivion is one of [[#Soul Strengths|six possible strengths]]: [[:Category:Oblivion-Souls-Petty|Petty]], [[:Category:Oblivion-Souls-Lesser|Lesser]], [[:Category:Oblivion-Souls-Common|Common]], [[:Category:Oblivion-Souls-Greater|Greater]], [[:Category:Oblivion-Souls-Grand|Grand]], and [[:Category:Oblivion-Souls-Black|Black]]. More powerful creatures have more powerful souls.  Souls are trapped in [[Oblivion:Soul Gem|soul gems]] by using a [[Oblivion:Soul Trap|soul trap]] spell. Trapped souls are used to [[Oblivion:Enchanting|enchant]] and [[Oblivion:Enchanting#Recharging_Weapons|recharge]] items.

==Souls Alphabetically==
This table provides soul strengths for most of the '''standard''' creatures in Oblivion. Unique creatures (like the lich [[OB:Undead#Lorgren Benirus|Lorgren Benirus]]) generally have leveled souls that are not in this table.  See individual [[OB:Creatures|creature]] pages for full details on their souls.

Souls with a superscript (e.g., <sup>L:-1</sup>) are leveled; see the [[#Soul Strengths|later section]] for details.  In a few cases (Ogres, Sanctified Spectre) there are several different versions of the creature sharing the same name but having different souls; see those creatures' pages for details.

==Soul Strengths==
There are six possible soul strengths:

A creature's soul strength is determined in one of two ways.

#Most standard creatures have fixed souls. For example, every Flame Atronach will have a common soul, regardless of location or player character's level.
#Most unique creatures and some other creatures have a soul strength based upon the player character's level--either the PC's level or an offset of the PC's level.

Offsets as low as -10 and as high as +10 exist, but the table below illustrates some common offsets:

| Greater || 16-20 || 15-19 || 14-18 || '''13-17''' || 12-16 || 11-15

| Grand || 21+ || 20+ || 19+ || '''18+''' || 17+ || 16+

Examples:
* When the PC is level 4, a Bloody Hand Rat Farmer (offset L:-3) will have a petty soul and all other Bloody Hand goblins (offsets L:-1 to L:+2) will have lesser souls.
* When the PC is level 5, a Bloody Hand Goblin War Chief (offset L:+2) will have a common soul and all other Bloody Hand goblins (offsets L:-3 to L:+1) will have lesser souls.  


An additional complication is that some creatures have a minimum or maximum level that also caps their soul.

For Instance:
* Ghosts have a '''maximum''' level of 4--their souls will be at most lesser strength. With an offset of L:-1, ghosts will have petty souls until PC level 3 and lesser souls at PC level 4 and above. This is summarized in the chart by "<=Lesser<sup>L:-1</sup>".
* Ogres have a '''minimum''' level of 16--their souls will be at least greater strength. With an offset of L:-3, ogres will have a greater soul until PC level 21 when their souls will be grand.  This is summarized in the chart by ">=Greater<sup>L:-3</sup>"

Creatures with no maximum or minimum soul level (any soul strength is possible) are stated with "leveled" followed by a superscript providing the offset.
