==Quick Walkthrough==
# Speak with [[Oblivion:Varulae|Varulae]] on the deck of [[Oblivion:The Serpent's Wake|The Serpent's Wake]] in [[Oblivion:Anvil|Anvil]] Docks.
# She asks you to retrieve her [[Oblivion:Varulae's Crystal Ball|crystal ball]] from the cargo hold of the haunted ship.
# Defeat the [[Oblivion:Spectral Sailor|Spectral Sailors]] or run past them, making your way down to the hold.
# Return Varulae's crystal ball to her, and she rewards you with the enchanted cutlass, [[Oblivion:Redwave|Redwave]].

==Detailed Walkthrough==
===The Serpent's Wake===
On the west side of [[Oblivion:Anvil|Anvil]] Docks, there's a ship called [[Oblivion:The Serpent's Wake|The Serpent's Wake]]. Speak with [[Oblivion:Varulae|Varulae]], who is standing on the main deck. She will tell you that the ship has recently returned from [[Lore:Summerset Isle|Summerset Isle]] with a family heirloom of hers, a [[Oblivion:Varulae's Crystal Ball|crystal ball]]. Unfortunately, the crew of the ship has been murdered and their ghosts now haunt the lower decks. Varulae will ask you to go down to the cargo hold and recover her crystal ball. When you agree to help, you're given a [[Oblivion:Serpent's Wake Key|key]] so that you may enter. You can either run this quest quickly, without a fight, or stand against five [[Oblivion:Spectral Sailor|Spectral Sailors]]; as with all ghosts, they are [[Oblivion:Resist Normal Weapons|immune to normal weapons]]. Inside the first door is a ghost that will sometimes stay in the room, so you can use a ranged spell to kill it easily, even at a low level. Once a ghost dies it leaves a [[Oblivion:Sharpened Cutlass|sharpened cutlass]], which can be used to fight other ghosts.

===The Anvil Harbor Massacre===
Inside the ship there is a trap door to the mid deck on the floor to your left, and a normal door in front of you. There's a Spectral Sailor behind the door in front, but some nice treasure too. If you decide to enter the room up ahead, make sure you search the "Dead Captain" to find the [[Oblivion:Serpent's Wake Captain's Key|Serpent's Wake Captain's Key]] and open the Captain's Chest at the foot of the bed to get the randomly generated treasure. Proceed to the mid deck through the trap door. There are two Spectral Sailors here, and they'll most likely attack you at once. You can fight or run down the corridor to another trap door leading to the cargo hold. There's nothing of interest in the mid deck anyway.

===Forget the Crew, Get the Ball===
Downstairs, there's a Chest just next to where you enter, and in it is 'Varulae's Crystal Ball' that you're after. The last two Spectral Sailors are here as well. There's no need to fight them if you choose, although there is a bottle of Skooma and some gems behind them. Retrace your steps back to the main deck, and speak with Varulae again to give her back her crystal ball and receive [[Oblivion:Redwave|Redwave]], an enchanted cutlass, as your reward.

==Notes==
* In the quest [[Oblivion:Following a Lead|Following a Lead]] you will find the ''[[Oblivion:Traitor's Diary|Traitor's Diary]]'', which reveals what happened to the ship's crew.
