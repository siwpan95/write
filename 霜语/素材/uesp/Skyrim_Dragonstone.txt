The '''Dragonstone''' is retrieved from the boss [[Skyrim:Draugr|Draugr]] in [[Skyrim:Bleak Falls Barrow (place)|Bleak Falls Barrow]]. On its front is a map showing various locations with [[Skyrim:Dragon Mounds|dragon burial sites]], and on its back is a script in the [[Lore:Dragon Alphabet|dragon alphabet]].
