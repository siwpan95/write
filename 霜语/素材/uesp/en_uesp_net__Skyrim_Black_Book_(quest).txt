== Quick Walkthrough ==
# Be approached by a [[Skyrim:Madman|madman]] ranting about a black book.
# Kill the madman when he turns hostile and find his [[Skyrim:Scribbles of a Madman|note]] revealing the book's location.
# Retrieve the black book from the dungeon.

== Detailed Walkthrough ==
In your travels across [[Skyrim:Solstheim|Solstheim]] you may come across a [[Skyrim:Madman|madman]] shouting things related to some [[Skyrim:Black Book (book)|mysterious books]]. He has a chance to spawn at several specific spots all over the island of Solstheim as part of a [[Skyrim:World Interactions#Black Book|world interaction]]. After a short conversation your map will be updated with the location of a Black Book and the madman will attack you. After defeating the madman you will need to travel to the location on your map to retrieve the Black Book.

Possible locations you can be sent to include: [[Skyrim:Benkongerike|Benkongerike]], [[Skyrim:Kolbjorn Barrow|Kolbjorn Barrow]], [[Skyrim:Raven Rock Mine|Raven Rock Mine]],{{vn}} [[Skyrim:Tel Mithryn (tower)|Tel Mithryn Tower]], and [[Skyrim:White Ridge Barrow|White Ridge Barrow]].

If the madman dies before he finishes the conversation, this quest will not be added unless you read the note on his body.

== Notes ==
*The madman will not approach you if you have already collected all of the [[Skyrim:Black Book (book)|Black Books]].
*If the book you are sent to collect is the one in [[Skyrim:Tel Mithryn (tower)|Tel Mithryn]], you will not be able to access it until you've done a number of quests for [[Skyrim:Neloth|Neloth]], so this quest may remain open for a while.
