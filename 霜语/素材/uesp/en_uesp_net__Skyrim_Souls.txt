Every '''[[Lore:Souls|soul]]''' in Skyrim is one of six possible strengths: [[:Category:Skyrim-Souls-Petty|Petty]], [[:Category:Skyrim-Souls-Lesser|Lesser]], [[:Category:Skyrim-Souls-Common|Common]], [[:Category:Skyrim-Souls-Greater|Greater]], [[:Category:Skyrim-Souls-Grand|Grand]], and [[:Category:Skyrim-Souls-Black|Black]]. More powerful creatures have more powerful souls. When a creature or NPC dies under a [[Skyrim:Soul Trap (effect)|Soul Trap]] effect, its soul is captured in the smallest sufficiently-sized [[Skyrim:Soul Gems|soul gem]] in the player's inventory. A soul may fill a gem larger than itself, which may be undesirable if one is in short supply of large gems. Filled soul gems may be used for [[Skyrim:Enchanting|enchanting]] items and [[Skyrim:Enchanting#Recharging_Weapons|recharging weapons]].

All NPCs, [[Skyrim:Dremora|Dremora]], and [[Skyrim:Ghost|ghosts]] have Black souls, which are as powerful as Grand souls but only fit in Black soul gems or [[Skyrim:The Black Star (item)|The Black Star]]. The soul level of any other creature is instead determined by its level as listed below; most creatures have a static level, but certain "leveled" creatures have a level equal to the player's level, possibly with a modifier (i.e. a multiplier or offset) applied.
