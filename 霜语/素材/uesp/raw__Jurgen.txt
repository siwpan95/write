'''Jurgen Windcaller''' is a [[Skyrim:Nord|Nord]] hero who can be found in [[Skyrim:Sovngarde (place)|Sovngarde]]. He wears [[Skyrim:Greybeard's Robes|Greybeard's robes]] along with a matching pair of [[Skyrim:Greybeard's Boots|boots]].

A prominent war leader in the [[Lore:First Empire of the Nords|First Empire of the Nords]], Jurgen Windcaller used his voice as a weapon and a way to grow in strength, like many Tongues of the time. This changed for Jurgen, however, when the Nordic armies suffered an ignominious defeat at [[Lore:Red Mountain|Red Mountain]]. Jurgen would use the defeat as inspiration to discover the [[Lore:Way of the Voice|Way of the Voice]] and found the [[Skyrim:Greybeards|Greybeards]].

For more information, see the [[Lore:Jurgen Windcaller|lore article]].

==Dialogue==
When spoken to, Jurgen may comment on his devout followers, the Greybeards.
:''"My disciples still follow the difficult path - the Way of the Voice is neither wide, nor easy. But if you stray from wisdom then to Sovngarde you will not return."'' 

He will also warn you:
:''"Fate drives you, but you follow your own path. Choose wisely, lest you wander into evil."''

==Notes==
* You must retrieve the [[Skyrim:Horn of Jurgen Windcaller|Horn of Jurgen Windcaller]] from his tomb in the Nordic ruin [[Skyrim:Ustengrav|Ustengrav]] during the quest [[Skyrim:The Horn of Jurgen Windcaller|The Horn of Jurgen Windcaller]].
* When the player approaches his grave after taking the horn, they can absorb a dragon soul from it. What this means is unknown.
* The inscription on his tomb in Ustengrav is "Windcaller" in [[Lore:Daedric Alphabet|Daedric]].
