[[Skyrim:Ainethach|Ainethach]] is the owner of the whole town including the two surrounding mines. The mines at Karthwasten are two of the few mines in the Reach that are not owned by a [[Skyrim:Nord|Nord]].

When you first enter Karthwasten, you will see the townsfolk squaring off against a group of mercenaries sent by the [[Skyrim:Thonar Silver-Blood#Silver-Blood Family|Silver-Blood family]] in [[Skyrim:Markarth|Markarth]] to stop them from working [[Skyrim:Sanuarach Mine|Sanuarach Mine]] until Ainethach agrees to sell the land to them.

The town consists of three buildings in a semicircle: [[Skyrim:Karthwasten Hall|Karthwasten Hall]], where Ainethach lives, [[Skyrim:Enmon's House|Enmon's house]], where [[Skyrim:Enmon|Enmon]] lives with his wife [[Skyrim:Mena|Mena]] and daughter [[Skyrim:Fjotra|Fjotra]], and the [[Skyrim:Karthwasten Miner's Barracks|miner's barracks]], where [[Skyrim:Belchimac|Belchimac]], [[Skyrim:Lash gra-Dushnikh|Lash gra-Dushnikh]], and [[Skyrim:Ragnar|Ragnar]] live. There is a [[Skyrim:Tanning Rack|tanning rack]] in front of Karthwasten Hall and a [[Skyrim:Grindstone|grindstone]] on the deck in front of the miner's barracks.

Sanuarach Mine is the main [[Skyrim:Silver|silver]] mine. Its only entrance is north of Karthwasten Hall. In front of the entrance is a [[Skyrim:Smelter|smelter]], three pieces of [[Skyrim:Silver Ore|silver ore]] in a cart opposite, and a [[Skyrim:Juniper Berries|Juniper]] tree on a rock. The entrance to [[Skyrim:Fenn's Gulch Mine|Fenn's Gulch Mine]] is at the northwestern end of town and has another smelter, two [[Skyrim:Silver Ingot|silver ingots]], and a piece of silver ore to the left of the entrance, with another Juniper tree to the right of the entrance.
